# Qu Care Agent

![tag:innovationlab](https://img.shields.io/badge/innovationlab-3D8BD3)
![tag:hackathon](https://img.shields.io/badge/hackathon-5F43F1)

Qu is a communication device for people who have lost speech (stroke, aphasia): they point a ring camera at
something, pick what they mean, and Qu says it in their own voice. This agent is the caregiver's side of Qu.

**Ask me (as the caregiver):**

- "How's Dad doing today?" – pain reports, when his as-needed medicine was last taken, what he said with Qu.
- "What medicines is he on?" – regular medicines, allergies, and the as-needed medicine status.
- "Tell him I'll be there at 5" – I have it read aloud, in a calm voice, on his Qu.

**I act on my own when he reports pain:** I check his medicines (Meds agent, FinchNode health record),
decide whether to escalate, and text you (Notify agent, iMessage via Photon).

Qu is not a medical device. I report what the health record and dose log say and leave decisions to people.
